## Hook (first 3 seconds)
- Open on the claim or the number, not "Hey everyone". First graphic lands on the first strong word.

## Pace
- Social presets: dead air ≤ 0.25s, a new graphic every 2–3s.
- Course preset: let it breathe (≤ 0.5s), graphics only where they teach.

## Captions
- 2–4 words, active word highlighted, never over the face.

## Graphic patterns
- Stat card for every number that matters.
- Numbered list when you say "three things", reveal each item on its word.
- Keyword chip for the one idea you want remembered.
