# Brand

Who's on camera: Jonathan Buckelew, co-founder of CRE AI Studio. Operator voice: 10+ years of multifamily asset management, now the go-to voice on AI in commercial real estate. Peer to the viewer, never a lecturer.

Audience: CRE operators, asset managers, analysts, brokers and investors who know NOI, cap rates and T12s cold, and want to know what AI actually changes in their workflow.

Look (machine-readable values live in `brand.json`; keep the two in sync):
- Font: Inter (700–800 for headlines, 500–600 for labels). Captions: Arial bold.
- Colours: CRE AI Studio blue #2563EB (accent, numbers, highlighted words), ink #0F172A (text), white cards, light blue tint #DBEAFE (kickers).
- Graphics are clean white cards with soft shadow and rounded corners. One idea per card. Numbers big, labels small.
- No stock-footage clichés, no emoji walls, no neon. Investor-grade, not hype.

Language on screen:
- Use real CRE terms correctly and in their usual case: NOI, T12, rent roll, cap rate, DSCR, IRR, LTV, OpEx, loss-to-lease, Yardi, RealPage.
- Numbers always with their unit (6.1% cap rate, $1.2M NOI, 94% occupancy).
- Logo/handle: add `brand.json → logo` (path to a PNG in references/) when ready.
